Privacy policy

Effective 11 November 2026

Tidepool is a single-player puzzle game. It has no accounts and no sign-in, and the developer does not collect, store or sell your personal information. This page explains the little data that is involved and where it goes.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (completed pools and pearls), your sound and vibration settings, and a few game preferences are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Anonymous player count

So the developer can see roughly how many people are playing, the app creates a random number the first time it opens. It is not your name, your Apple ID or your device's advertising identifier, and it can't be used to identify you. While the game is open, it sends that random number, the highest set you've reached and the app version to the developer's database (hosted by Supabase) every couple of minutes. These are only used to count players and are never shared, sold or used for advertising or tracking. Deleting the app deletes the random number.

Advertising

Tidepool is free because it shows occasional ads between pools. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking),
  • your IP address, and an approximate location worked out from it,
  • information about the ads shown and whether they were tapped,
  • basic device and diagnostic information.

When the app first opens, iPhone asks whether Tidepool may track you. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, your advertising identifier is not shared, and you'll still see ads, just less personalised ones. You can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

If you're in the UK, the European Economic Area or Switzerland, you'll also be asked for consent through Google's consent form before any personalised ads are shown.

Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences. Google's use of this information is covered by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy, and how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

The optional Remove ads purchase is handled entirely by Apple. The developer never sees your payment details or Apple ID.

Children

Tidepool is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Turn off tracking in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Reset your advertising identifier in your iPhone's settings.
  • Buy Remove ads to stop ads, and the ad data collection that comes with them, entirely.
  • Delete the app to remove everything stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
