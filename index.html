A calm ripple puzzle

Tap the water. The ripple spreads, the stones drift, and one by one they settle into their hollows. Find the few taps that guide every stone home.

100 pools across 10 sets, each adding a new idea: rocks, coloured stones, lily pads, reeds, currents, whirlpools, linked wells and song pools. No timers, no lives.
