Support

Stuck on a pool, found a bug, or have an idea? Email poxysstudios@gmail.com and you'll get a reply, usually within a few days. It helps to mention your iPhone model and the pool number.

Common questions

I'm stuck on a pool. Tap the hint button (the rings at the top) and a soft marker shows where to tap next. Using a hint caps that pool at two pearls.

How do I remove ads? Tap the heart on the title screen and choose Remove ads. It's a one-time purchase, and every pool stays free either way.

I bought Remove ads but I'm seeing ads again. Tap the heart on the title screen, then Restore purchase. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

Where is my progress saved? On your phone. Deleting the app deletes your progress; your Remove ads purchase is kept by Apple and can be restored.

Can I turn off sound or vibration? Yes, with the two buttons on the title screen.
